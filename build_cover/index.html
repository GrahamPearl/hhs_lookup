⚡ Quick Navigation Guide
📋 Workflow Steps
Step 1: Select Day
Choose the day to manage covers. All operations apply to this day.
Step 2: Add Absences
Click Add (or search) to open the absence modal. Choose Full Day or Partial, select the reason, and confirm.
Step 3: Auto-Assign
Choose a strategy (Fair, Round-Robin, or Day-Balancing) and preview assignments before applying.
Step 4: Apply & Export
Confirm your plan and export to Excel, PDF, or email. Save progress to localStorage.
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
Ctrl + 1-4 Jump to workflow step
Ctrl + → Next workflow step
Ctrl + ← Previous workflow step
Alt + A Run auto-assign preview
Alt + E Export to Excel
💡 Tips for Speed:
  • Use keyboard shortcuts to navigate between steps quickly
  • 🟡 Gold badge — teacher has only one free period left today
  • 🟠 Orange border — teacher is approaching their daily or weekly cover cap
  • 🔴 Red border — teacher has reached their daily or weekly cover cap
  • ↩ Cyan border — teacher covered on the prior calendar day
  • D / W / A / F badges show daily, weekly, average and free-period counts
  • Search for absent teachers by typing — filtered results appear instantly
  • Use the Dashboard Summary Widget to track fairness in real-time
