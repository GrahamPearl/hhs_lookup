3. Seating layout (S-snake pattern)

Internal candidates are seated first, filling whole columns in S-snake order. External candidates always start at the front/top of a brand-new column — they are never added to a partially-used internal column.

Unassigned candidates

Click a name to select it, close this window, then click an empty seat to place them. This is a manual override — the internal/external column rule is not enforced here.

5. Preview & export

"Preview as image" and "Download image(s) (PNG)" split wide layouts into a numbered series of pictures, 5 columns of desks per picture, so each one prints cleanly on its own page. "Download single image (PNG)" instead produces one picture with every column of the seating plan, however wide it is. External candidates are always shown with the amber "EXT" marker, in exports as well as on screen.
