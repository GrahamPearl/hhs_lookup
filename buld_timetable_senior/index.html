Senior Management Help
What’s new in Senior Management Mode?
  • Bulk Upload: Import a folder of teacher timetable JSON files.
  • Identity Tools: Rename teachers and remove titles in bulk.
  • Bulk DND: Mark meeting periods as Do Not Disturb for selected teachers.
  • Last Resort: Flag teachers who may be used only when no standard teachers are available.
  • Completeness Tools: Detect and auto-fill uncaptured cells without overwriting existing entries.
Policy reminders
  • DND meetings are never eligible for cover allocation.
  • Last Resort teachers are only used when there are no other eligible teachers.
Note
This interface edits authoritative timetable data. Cover planning is performed separately in the Cover Management Dashboard.
